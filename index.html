Research & publications

At University of Malaya Medical Centre, I brought together data from more than 10 sources to help build the MeLODY diabetes database and cleaned and analysed Malaysia’s National Diabetes Registry, involving 1.6 million patients.

I also contributed to communicating research findings through a workshop with the University of Oxford and Malaysia’s Ministry of Health. The publications below are collaborative studies that I co-authored.

  • Health poverty among people with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) in Malaysia Social Science & Medicine · 2024
  • Effects of mobile health interventions on health-related outcomes in older adults with type 2 diabetes: A systematic review and meta-analysis Journal of Diabetes · 2023
